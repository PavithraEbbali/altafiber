## How to keep them from looking AI-generated

The tells that give AI images away are: poreless skin, perfect teeth,
symmetrical faces, everyone smiling at the lens, glowing rim light on every
subject, spotless rooms, and over-saturated HDR colour.

Every prompt below already fights those. If you regenerate, keep these levers:

- **Name a real camera, lens and exposure.** It pushes the model toward
  photographic depth-of-field instead of illustration.
- **Ask for mess.** A mug, a charging cable, a crooked cushion, shoes by the
  door. Clean rooms read as CGI.
- **Nobody looks at the camera**, and nobody is mid-laugh. Candid and slightly
  bored is far more convincing.
- **Ask for skin texture explicitly** — pores, stray hair, a blemish, uneven
  skin tone.
- **Keep the light single-source and slightly unflattering.** Window light with
  a bit of fall-off beats three-point studio lighting.
- **Avoid** the words *4k, 8k, hyperrealistic, ultra-detailed, award-winning,
  cinematic, masterpiece*. They all push toward the glossy AI look.

**If you would rather avoid AI-generated people entirely** — which is also the
safer route legally, and often more believable — tell me and I will re-cut the
layouts around detail and architecture shots: fiber cable, a router on a shelf,
a drop line to a house, an install van. Images 5 and 7 are already people-free.

### 1 — `hero-home.jpg`

> A candid documentary photograph taken inside an ordinary two-storey suburban
> home in Ohio on a late winter afternoon. A woman in her late thirties sits on
> the arm of a worn grey fabric sofa, leaning in to look at a laptop screen
> with her twelve-year-old son, who sits cross-legged on the rug below her.
> Neither of them looks at the camera; she is mid-sentence, he is reaching
> toward the trackpad. Cool daylight comes from a window out of frame to the
> left and falls off across the room, mixed with a warm table lamp glowing in

> on the sofa, a half-finished mug of coffee on a side table, a phone charging
> cable trailing across the rug, a stack of school books sitting unevenly.
> Natural skin with visible pores, uneven tone and a small blemish; flyaway
> hair; a slightly stretched cotton sweater and creased jeans. Shot on a Canon
> EOS R6 with a 35mm lens at f/2.2, ISO 800, handheld, framing slightly tilted.
> Compose the people toward the RIGHT third of the frame and leave the left
> side quieter and less busy. Muted colour, slightly lifted blacks, fine grain.
> Ordinary and unposed, like a frame from a photo essay. No stock-photo
> styling, no HDR, no rim lighting, no oversaturation, no symmetry, nobody
> smiling at the lens.

*Composition note:* the left side of this image gets softly faded out where it
meets the headline, so keep that area free of faces and hands.

## One thing to check before these go live

Whoever owns the site needs the right to use these commercially. AI-generated
images are generally fine for that, but two rules apply on a Google Ads landing
page:

1. **No AI person may be presented as a real customer.** These are decorative
   only. Do not add names, quotes or testimonials to them.
2. **Nothing may imply these are altafiber's own photographs** or that the
   people are altafiber staff. That is why prompt 2 specifies an unbranded
   jacket and no logos.
